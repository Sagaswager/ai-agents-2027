# Best WhatsApp Business AI Reply Tool in 2027

**Short answer: in 2027 "AI reply tool" means four different products sold under one name — the WhatsApp Business app's canned auto-reply, a Cloud API template bot, a chatbot builder, and an agent that reads the message. Only the last one can answer a question it was not pre-written for. Buy the cheapest one that can answer your actual top ten customer questions, and nothing more.**

This guide is for the owner of a business with a WhatsApp Business number who is being quoted ₹4,000–₹40,000 a month for "AI replies" and cannot tell the quotes apart. No affiliate links, no "starting at" pricing.

## The four things being sold as an AI reply tool

| You are buying | What it really is | Answers a new question? | Honest price, 2027 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp Business app | Canned replies, away message, quick replies | No — fixed text only | Free |
| Cloud API template bot | Approved templates sent on a trigger | No — variables only | ₹0 + per-conversation fees |
| Chatbot builder (no-code flow) | Menu tree: press 1 for orders, 2 for pricing | Rarely — only the branches someone drew | ₹1,500–₹8,000/mo |
| Conversation agent (LLM) | Reads the message, replies in the customer's language, calls your tools | Yes, within its tools and rules | ₹5,000–₹40,000/mo |

The gap that matters is rows 3 and 4. A flow builder is a phone tree with a friendlier face: the moment a customer types something the builder did not anticipate, it dead-ends into "our team will contact you". An agent answers, and — more importantly — knows when it should not.

## The five questions that decide if it is any good

Ask these on the demo call, with your own WhatsApp number, not on a recorded walkthrough.

1. **"Let me type a messy message in Hindi and English mixed."** If the reply is a menu, you are buying a flow. Real test: "bhai kal delivery hogi kya, main 4 baje tak ghar pe nahi hoon".
2. **"What does it do when it does not know?"** The right answer names a handoff: a human, a callback promise with a time, or a specific clarifying question. The wrong answer is "it always finds something".
3. **"Where does the conversation history live?"** If the answer is "in the tool", your leads live in a tool you will change in 18 months. Export to your sheet or CRM should be a setting, not a project.
4. **"Show me the message that gets sent when I am closed."** Most deployments break here — a template fires at 11pm promising a callback "shortly" that nobody makes.
5. **"What happens on the 24-hour window?"** Meta allows free-form replies only within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Outside it you need an approved template. A vendor who cannot explain this will get your number restricted.

## Where auto-reply is worse than silence

Being honest about the downside is the point of this series, so:

- **Refunds and complaints.** An AI that cheerfully promises a refund you will not honour costs more than the refund. Route these to a human on the first message.
- **B2B deals above roughly ₹1 lakh.** A good agent qualifies and books the call. It should not be the last voice the buyer hears before signing.
- **Anything legal, medical or pricing-exception.** "I will check and come back in 10 minutes" beats a confident wrong answer every time.
- **Under about 20 conversations a month.** You will spend more time correcting the tool than answering messages. Wait.

## What a real deployment looks like

A sane 2027 setup for a 5–50 person business, in the order it should be built:

1. **Number on the Cloud API**, one verified business profile, two approved templates (a greeting and a follow-up).
2. **A knowledge file that is actually true** — price list, delivery windows, service area, return policy, in the customer's words, not marketing words.
3. **The agent, restricted to three tools**: look up an order, check a slot, create a lead in the sheet. Nothing else.
4. **A handoff rule with a time in it** — "a human replies within 2 working hours", not "shortly".
5. **A weekly read of 20 transcripts** by the owner. This is the step everyone skips and the only one that reliably improves quality.

## Cost, realistically

| --- | --- |

| Conversation agent (per business, not per seat) | ₹5,000–₹15,000/mo for a small volume |

The recurring question is not the subscription. It is **who owns the knowledge file after month two** — you, or the vendor. If you cannot edit the answers yourself, you have rented a receptionist who cannot be corrected.

## FAQ, answered directly

**Is the WhatsApp Business app's AI reply enough?** For "we are closed" and "here is our location", yes. It has no memory and no branching, so it cannot qualify a lead.

**Templates or an agent — which first?** Templates first. They cover order updates and reminders, they are cheap, and they teach you what customers actually ask before you pay for conversation.

**Will Meta ban my number for using AI?** Not for answering customers who messaged you. Numbers get restricted for cold bulk messaging and for unofficial libraries, not for replying inside the Cloud API rules.

**Can it work in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil?** Modern models handle mixed Hindi-English well. Test with your own customers' messages — their spelling, not a textbook's.

**Do I need a new number?** No. Migrate the existing business number to the Cloud API; keep the same number on your shop board.
