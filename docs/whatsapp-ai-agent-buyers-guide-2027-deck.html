Buyer's Guide 2027

What an Indian SMB actually pays, what actually gets delivered, and the five questions that kill a bad quote.

The short answer

There is no "₹X per month"

A WhatsApp AI agent in India is billed as three separate things. Any single monthly number you are quoted is one third of the answer.

1. Platform

The tool or the agency. Roughly ₹0–₹3,000/month for an SMB, sometimes a one-time setup instead.

2. Meta

Per-24-hour conversation or per template message, billed in USD, moved with the rupee.

3. AI usage

Per token or per reply. Invisible until someone forwards 4,000 messages.

Ask for all three, at your own volume, in writing.

Worked example

2,000 conversations a month, one number

Assumptions, so you can change them

500 marketing template conversations you start; 1,500 service conversations the customer starts inside the 24-hour window; an AI agent replying to most of them.

What it becomes

Platform: ₹2,000–₹3,000. Meta: the cheap end for inbound, the expensive end for templates — roughly five to seven times per message. AI: the line nobody quotes, and the one that grows with success.

Change one assumption and the total moves more than you expect. That is why a flat quote with no volume question is a guess.

Inbound support and broadcast marketing are different cost shapes.

What moves the bill

Five things, in order of surprise

  1. Who starts the conversation. Customer-initiated is far cheaper than a template you send.
  2. Language and code-mixing. Hinglish, Marathi, Tamil replies cost more tokens than clipped English.
  3. Memory. Remembering a customer's budget across weeks is a feature and a line item.
  4. Integrations. CRM sync, calendar booking, payment links — tool calls bill like text.
  5. Retries and fallbacks. An agent that re-asks or escalates does more work than one that answers once.
None of these are scams. All of them are invisible in a quote.

Red flags

Five lines that should end the meeting

01One number, no volume question. They have not modelled your usage.

02"Unlimited messages." Nobody can sell Meta's fee as unlimited. Check whether they mean platform messages only.

03No answer at 3× your volume. The bill is not the number, the curve is.

04A per-seat price for a chatbot. Seats matter for humans reading transcripts, not the agent.

05Silence on ownership. Who keeps the number, the WABA and the prompt library if you leave? That is the expensive part of switching.

Ask each one in the first call. A good vendor answers plainly.

Not everyone should buy

When an AI agent is the wrong purchase

Under ~30 real enquiries a month

A saved-replies catalogue and one attentive human cost less and answer better.

Prices not written down anywhere

An agent will confidently quote the wrong one. Fix the source of truth first, then automate.

Nobody will read the first 100 transcripts

Then you are paying for a system that repeats its own mistakes quietly.

Saying this is how you tell a vendor from a salesperson.

The cheapest legitimate path

Three months, in this order

Month 1

Official Cloud API number, a small agent on the FAQ set, every conversation hands over to a human. Measure deflection and reply time.

Month 2

Add the two flows you actually saw in transcripts. Nothing else.

Month 3

Only now decide whether a custom build is worth it.

Most Indian SMBs never need to leave step 2. The ones who do know exactly why.

Free gets you the channel, not the agent: the AI usage and the setup work are still yours.
